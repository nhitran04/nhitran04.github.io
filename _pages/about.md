My name is Nhi Tran. I am an incoming Ph.D. Computer Science student starting this fall at [to be announced 😆] and a Research Intern at the [Navy Center for Applied Research in Artificial Intelligence (NCARAI)](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/itd/aic/content/about-ncarai/) at the [U.S. Naval Research Laboratory](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/), where I am being advised by [Dr. David Porfirio](https://dporfirio.github.io/). In my free time, I collaborate with [Dr. Snehesh Shrestha](https://www.snehesh.com/) from the [University of Maryland](https://umd.edu/) (UMD) and the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://www.nist.gov/) (NIST) on human-robot interaction research.

I received my B.S. in [Computer Science](https://www.cs.umd.edu/) and minor in [Geographic Information Science](https://geog.umd.edu/) from UMD in the Fall of 2023 (go terps! 🐢). During my undergrad, I participated in the [Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program](https://www.navalsteminterns.us/nreip/) (NREIP) at NRL as a Research and Development Intern under the mentorship of [Dr. "Mak" Roberts](https://makro-nrl.bitbucket.io/), where I spearheaded efforts to automate logistics planning for military operations. At UMD, I assisted with research labs across disciplines, including but not limited to human-computer interaction, network security, and civil and environmental engineering. These experiences have allowed me to apply my diverse skillsets to my research and collaborations.

Broadly, I am interested in **artificial intelligence**, **planning**, **robotics**, and **human-robot interaction** research. I aim to develop algorithms that make robots more intelligent, thereby augmenting human decision-making during human-robot collaborations and improving trust in robots.
