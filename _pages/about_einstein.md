Broadly, I am interested in **planning** and **human-robot interaction** research. I aim to develop algorithms that make robots more intelligent, thereby augmenting human decision-making during human-robot collaborations. Currently at NRL, I am investigating different representations for capturing human intent when specifying tasks for a robot by comparing goals (i.e., the partial state of the world that the robot strives to achieve) versus actions (i.e., what the robot does to achieve a state of the world). At UMD, I am assisting with developing a framework that creates scalable, flexible, and immersive human-robot interaction experimental platforms that allow researchers to collect diverse interaction datasets.
